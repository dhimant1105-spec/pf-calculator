Wage ceilings and EDLI

VPF is not a rate. It is an amount entered per employee in the Employees page and per month in the wage entry. Draft months use these rates as they stand. A finalised month keeps the rates it was closed with.

How each figure is worked out

MPF = PF wages × MPF %. Non-pension members use the non-pension rate. Pension members use their own MPF % if one is set on the employee, otherwise the pension-member rate.
VPF = the amount entered for the month, in rupees. The ECR employee column carries MPF + VPF.
EPS = EPS wages × EPS % for pension members, where EPS wages are PF wages up to the EPS ceiling. Non-pension members have no EPS wages and no EPS.
CPF (employer, difference column) = PF wages × employer share − EPS. For a pension member on wages up to the ceiling this is 3.67%. A non-pension member has no EPS to deduct, so the whole employer share is CPF.
EDLI wages = PF wages up to the EDLI ceiling. EDLI amount = EDLI wages × EDLI %. The amount belongs on the challan. The ECR file carries only the wages.
NCP days are days without pay. They go to the ECR as entered and do not change the wages here. Every amount is rounded to the nearest rupee.
